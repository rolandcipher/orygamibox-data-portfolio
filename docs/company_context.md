# OrygamiBox S.A. - company context (fictional)

OrygamiBox S.A. manufactures custom folding cartons and printed cardboard packaging for other companies (B2B). It started ten years ago as a family print shop making shoe boxes and grew into a small industrial company. Growth brought bottlenecks in the plant, scrap that is hard to trace, and customers who want shorter lead times on tight budgets.

## Customers

| Sector | What they buy |
|---|---|
| Cosmetics and personal care | Boxes for perfume, creams and serums, with high-end finishes (matte lamination, emboss, foil) |
| Food and beverage | Boxes for chocolate, tea, supplements and special bottles; food-grade board and inks |
| E-commerce and fashion | Strong, quick-to-assemble shipping boxes printed with the brand |
| Technology and electronics | Structured boxes with inserts that protect devices and cables |

## Order life cycle

```
Customer -> 1. Quote -> 2. Approval and design -> 3. Purchasing -> 4. Production -> 5. Delivery
```

1. **Sales:** the customer sends size, board, printing, finish and quantity; sales answers with a price.
2. **Engineering and design:** the cutting die and artwork are prepared once the quote is approved.
3. **Purchasing:** board, inks and varnish are checked in stock; if missing, a purchase order goes to a supplier.
4. **Plant:** printing, finishing (lamination, emboss, foil), die-cutting, gluing and quality control. Pieces that fail are scrapped.
5. **Logistics:** finished boxes are packed and dispatched to the customer.

## Who uses the data

| Area | Needs |
|---|---|
| General management | Margins, profitability by client |
| Plant | Machine time, downtime, scrap |
| Sales | Quote funnel, response times, sales by sector |
| Purchasing | Supplier lead times and prices |
